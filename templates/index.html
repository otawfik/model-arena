Replaying the same {{ "{:,}".format(board.meta.n_games) }} games in {{ board.meta.n_orders }} different orders moves online Elo by about ±{{ "%.0f"|format(top.elo_order_std) }} points per model — wider than Bradley–Terry’s entire 95% interval (±{{ "%.0f"|format((top.ci_high - top.ci_low) / 2) }}). Online Elo answers “who won recently”; Bradley–Terry answers “who is stronger”.
